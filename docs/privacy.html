CNS Pre-Bidding Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

The CNS Pre-Bidding application (“App”) helps authenticated users view pre-bid items and submit bids. This policy describes how account information is used.

Information we collect

  • Google account identifier, name, and email address used for sign-in (SSO).
  • Bids and related activity you submit in the App.
  • Administrator actions such as bid corrections or cancellations.

How we use information

  • Authenticate users and authorize Bidder / Administrator roles.
  • Process and display bidding activity.
  • Maintain an audit trail for fairness and support.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Bidder identities are hidden from other bidders and visible only to administrators as needed to run the event.

Data storage

Application data is stored by the App hosting environment used for the event. Google sign-in is handled by Google Identity; we receive basic profile claims needed for authentication.

Contact

Questions: contact the event administrator who shared the App link with you.
